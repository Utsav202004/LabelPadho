## LinkedIn

The popcorn pack said "Classic Butter" in big letters.

The ingredients list said butter: 0.16%.

That's the moment I decided to build something.

I've just launched **LabelPadho** (लेबल पढ़ो, "read the label"), a free tool that tells you what's really inside Indian packaged food:

→ Snap the label, scan the barcode, or just search the product
→ Get a health score out of 10, with every point explained
→ See which front-of-pack claims hold up, and which don't
→ Find hidden sugar, salt and palm oil, judged per 100g, so tiny "serving sizes" can't hide anything
→ Pick a profile (diabetic, high BP, gym, kids) for a personal verdict
→ Read it in Hindi with one tap

It already covers 200+ everyday Indian products, and every new scan is saved for everyone.

How I built it: I used AI coding agents (Claude Code and Antigravity) as my engineering team, while I owned the product, the decisions and the testing. Gemini reads the label photo, but every score and number is calculated by plain code, never guessed by the AI.

Inspired by the label-reading movement started by FoodPharmer and Glucose Gal, I wanted a tool anyone can use in a shop aisle in 10 seconds.

Try it on the snack in your kitchen and tell me what surprised you:
