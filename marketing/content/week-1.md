## Day 7: The trick behind small serving sizes (no brand)
Educational post that explains why LabelPadho judges food per 100g.

**X**
Ever noticed nutrition labels with tiny serving sizes?
A 150g chips pack can list values for 30g, so 300mg sodium looks fine. Eat the pack and it's 1,500mg, most of a day's limit.
That's why LabelPadho scores everything per 100g.
https://labelpadho.utsavgoyal.tech/?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=education

**LinkedIn**
One label trick, explained.
Brands choose the serving size. Pick a small one and every number looks small.
Example: a 150g chips pack listing 300mg sodium per 30g serving. The whole pack is 1,500mg, about 75% of the WHO daily limit.
So LabelPadho judges every product per 100g. Same yardstick for every brand, no matter what the label calls a serving.
Check the snacks in your kitchen:
https://labelpadho.utsavgoyal.tech/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=education
